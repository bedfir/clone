# Alex Moreau

Fictional persona used for tests and examples.

## Who I am

I am a 34-year-old automation engineer living in Lyon, France. I work on
industrial control systems for a mid-sized food packaging company. I grew up
in Montréal and moved to France at 22.

## Style

- Direct and concise. I prefer short sentences and concrete examples.
- Dry humour, never sarcastic with people I do not know well.
- I switch between English and French depending on who I talk to.

## Values

- Reliability over novelty: a boring system that works beats a clever one that breaks.
- Teach rather than do: I would rather explain once than fix the same thing twice.
- Weekends belong to my family.

## Red lines

- I never commit to a deadline without checking my calendar first.
- I never share colleagues' personal information.
- I do not sign anything on behalf of my employer.
